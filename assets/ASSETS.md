## 本次图像生成提示词

### 主菜单插画

Stylized concept art for the full-bleed main menu of an independent Chinese PC game about surviving an emergency landing on Mars. Landscape 16:10. Wide cinematic composition looking from a low ridge across a red ochre Martian basin. On the RIGHT HALF ONLY, a lone astronaut in a chalk-white hard-shell suit with a small cobalt visor faces a partly buried compact white lander with copper stripes, a dim cyan emergency light and scattered fragments. Dust plumes, distant cliffs, thin warm horizon. LEFT 42% intentionally very dark near-black eggplant rock in shadow, soft low-detail texture, reserved for UI title and buttons. Elegant painterly science fiction, hand-painted matte game illustration, grounded NASA-inspired hardware, somber but hopeful. Palette charcoal burgundy, burnt sienna, warm ivory, occasional oxidized teal. No words, no lettering, no watermarks, no UI. One coherent scene.

### 火星地表

Asset for a 2D PC Mars survival game: one landscape 16:10 playable map ground texture, directly overhead orthographic view, camera looking vertically down, zero perspective, no horizon or sky. Detailed but subdued hand-painted Martian regolith in dark desaturated warm red-browns with warm copper highlights, sandy streaks and a few clusters of dark rough rocks around the very edges. Left half and center open mostly smooth dust, slightly more textured rock formations along the upper and right edges. Mostly medium-dark, low visual noise, subtle light from upper left. Entire image continuous terrain. No landmarks, no buildings, no people, no artificial objects, no text, no UI, no grid. Authentic top-down game terrain plate.
